About the Website

Anime Recommendations was created to be calm, soft and sensory-friendly.

Simple navigation, easy reading, and a peaceful place to explore anime.

The goal was to make something cozy — a space where people don’t feel overwhelmed.

About the Creator

Hi! I'm Sofia, an anime enthusiast and accessibility advocate.

I created Anime Haven to combine two of my passions: storytelling through animation and inclusive web design.

I believe everyone deserves a space to explore what they love without stress or sensory overload.

This site is built with that care in mind — simple layouts, reduced motion options, and hand-curated recommendations.

With over five years of streaming, I finally decided to share my recommendations publicly.

I enjoy attending conventions, cosplaying different characters, and meeting new people.

I am a senior in university in the US and the founder of the anime society!

I hope this space helps you find your next favorite anime.
